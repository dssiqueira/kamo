# Code of Conduct

Kamo follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming, kind and constructive. Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, and publishing other people's private information are not acceptable, in any project space (issues, pull requests, discussions) or when representing the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it privately to the maintainers through GitHub: use the "Report content" option on the issue or comment, or open a private security advisory in this repository and mark it as a conduct report. Reports are handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that don't follow this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behavior they find inappropriate, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
