# Store listing

Name and short description come from `packages/i18n` (`extension.name`, `extension.description`,
≤ 132 characters) through `_locales/`. The long description below is pasted per language in the
developer dashboard. Languages: the five Kamo ships today (D-021). E-06 also lists German, French and
Korean: add them here when they land in `packages/i18n`.

## English (en)
Record any browser tab and get a video that looks edited.

Kamo zooms in on every click, smooths the cursor and puts your tab on a nice background, so a
quick recording comes out ready to share. No editing skills, no account, free.

• Record the current tab with its sound, your microphone and your webcam
• Automatic zoom on every click, with a camera that moves smoothly (never linear)
• A smooth cursor and a click ripple, redrawn over the video
• Edit in your browser: move or remove zooms, trim, change the background, padding and corners
• Webcam bubble or split layout, 16:9, 1:1 and 9:16
• Export MP4 (and GIF) straight to your downloads, no watermark
• Everything stays in your browser: no upload, no tracking, open source (MIT)

To record the whole screen or other apps, and for local transcription and captions, get the free
Kamo desktop app.

• 現在のタブを、タブの音声・マイク・Web カメラと一緒に録画

• なめらかなカーソルとクリックの波紋を動画の上に描き直し
