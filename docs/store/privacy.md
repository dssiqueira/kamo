# Kamo for Chrome — Privacy policy

_Last updated: 2026-11-04_

Kamo is a free, open-source screen recorder (MIT). This policy covers the Kamo extension for Chrome
and other Chromium browsers.

**Nothing leaves your browser unless you send it.**

- **What Kamo records:** only the tab you choose, only after you press Record, until you press Stop.
  If you turn them on, also your microphone and your camera.
- **Clicks:** while recording, Kamo listens to the mouse in the recorded tab (position, button,
  scroll) to zoom where you click. It never listens to the keyboard and never reads the page's
  content.
- **Where it is kept:** recordings, projects and exports are stored in your browser (its private file
  storage for the extension). Exported videos go to your downloads folder. Your settings are kept in
  the browser's extension storage.
- **What Kamo sends:** nothing. The extension has no analytics, no telemetry, no ads and no account.
  It makes no network request of its own.
- **Deleting your data:** delete a project in "All projects", or remove the extension (or clear its
  data) to delete everything it stored.
- **Kamo Cloud (later, optional):** a future version may let you send a video to Kamo Cloud. Only
  what you choose to send would be uploaded, and it would stay in your account. This policy will be
  updated before that ships.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/dssiqueira/kamo.
