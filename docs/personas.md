# personas.md

## Persona 1 — Marina, product PM
- **Context:** PM at a scale-up, records a demo of every new feature for release notes, Slack and LinkedIn
- **How she uses it:** records 1–3 min, adjusts 1 or 2 zooms, exports 16:9 and 1:1
- **Frequency:** 2–4 videos per week
- **What she values:** speed. Wants the video ready before the coffee gets cold
- **What she avoids:** opening a video editor, creating an account just to record, watermarks
- **Kamo Cloud:** sends the link in Slack and in the ticket; builds a release notes playlist
- **Cloud MCP:** asks the agent "build a playlist with this sprint's demos and give me the link for the release note"
- **Quote:** "I just wanted the video to not look like it was recorded on Zoom."

## Persona 2 — Rafa, AI content creator
- **Context:** posts short tutorials on Reels, TikTok, X and YouTube Shorts
- **How he uses it:** records, turns on automatic captions, webcam in the corner, exports 9:16 + 16:9
- **Frequency:** daily
- **What he values:** vertical format, captions, face on screen, 60fps
- **What he avoids:** paying for a feature that should be basic; yet another subscription without a clear reason
- **Quote:** "If the app is free and does everything, I tell everyone about it."
- **Kamo Cloud:** uses it little (posts straight to social networks)

## Persona 3 — Léo, open source dev
- **Context:** maintains a lib/tool and records GIFs/videos for the README and the Product Hunt launch
- **How he uses it:** short recordings, exports GIF and 1:1 MP4
- **What he values:** open source, no telemetry, privacy
- **What he avoids:** an app that asks for keyboard permission without explaining; a mandatory cloud
- **Kamo Cloud:** might use the link to put a demo in issues and PRs; respects that the cloud is optional
- **MCP:** the first to connect Claude Code to the local MCP ("find the recording where I reproduced the bug")
- **Quote:** "If it's MIT and runs local, I'll install it."

## Anti-persona
- Professional video editor who needs multi-track, color grading and fine cutting → goes to DaVinci
- Company that needs per-viewer analytics (who watched, how far), SSO and team administration → Loom
